Questions

Before you buy

Is it safe to leave burning overnight?
Never leave a burning coil unattended or where a child or pet can reach it. Place Breezeguard on a flat, hard surface at least 1 metre from beds, curtains, and walls — the unit is designed to stay cool to the touch so it is safer than a bare coil, but it still needs adult supervision.
Will it work with any mosquito coil?
Yes. The plate fits any standard 4.5-inch (11.4 cm) coil you can buy in the market, plus the smaller mosquito coils sold online. Our refill pack is optional — use whatever you already buy.
How long does one coil last?
Roughly 7–8 hours in Classic and 11–12 hours in Twin Duo, depending on room size, airflow, and how drafty the room is. In an enclosed room the fan moves more smoke upward, so it burns slightly slower.
What if it arrives broken?
Send us a photo within 7 days and we replace the unit immediately. We also accept 30-day returns for unused items in original packaging.
