Choose your method at checkout. You will see the exact amount — including delivery — before you confirm.

Pay from your GCash wallet. After you place the order we send a payment link to your email and mobile number — it is valid for 24 hours.

Available for Metro Manila, provincial, and pickup orders.

Maya wallet or a linked Maya/Visa card. Confirmation is usually instant, and your order is packed as soon as the payment lands.

One-tap checkout if you are signed in on your phone.
BPI Bank transfer (BPI, BDO, others)

We reserve your items for 24 hours while you transfer. Send the exact order total and put your order number as the reference.

VISA Credit / debit card

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?
Metro Manila orders arrive in 1–2 working days, provincial orders in 3–5 working days. Orders placed before 2 p.m. on a weekday are packed the same day. During typhoon season, allow an extra 1–2 days — courier suspensions are outside our control, and we do not charge extra for the delay.
Can I pay when the parcel arrives?
Yes, cash on delivery is available for Metro Manila destinations. There is a ₱50 COD fee on orders under ₱1,500, and it is waived entirely on orders above ₱1,500 because shipping is already free.
Do you have a store I can visit?
Our workshop at PHINMA-COC Campus in Cagayan de Oro City is open for pickup Monday to Saturday, 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Come by and see the colourways in person — we keep all four on the shelf.
Is mosquito coil safe for babies and pets?
Use coil repellent with extra care if there are young children, pregnant women, or birds in the room. Keep the unit at least 1 metre away from beds and never leave it unattended. Breezeguard keeps ash contained and the shell cool to the touch, but it is still a burning coil — supervise it like one.
What warranty do I get?
One year on the fan, USB port, and electronics. If any of these fail we repair or replace the unit — we keep spare fan boards in stock for years, so you do not need to buy a new device.
Do you offer bulk or reseller pricing?
Yes — five units or more gets wholesale pricing for hotels, boarding houses, and sari-sari store owners. Use the contact form and choose "Bulk or reseller order" as the topic.
