Why Classic works

A coil sitting on a table pushes most of its smoke straight up — so the room corner stays bite-free while the rest of the house does not. The pinwheel lid on Breezeguard fixes that: the fan draws the repellent through the vents and spreads it in a wide, even cone across roughly 15 square metres.

Because it runs off a 5V USB supply, you can also run it from a power bank for camping or during power outages — a real problem in typhoon season. The shell stays cool to the touch even after 8 hours, and the ash lip means you empty it once a week, not every night.
